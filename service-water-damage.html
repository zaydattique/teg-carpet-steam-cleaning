Good to know

Water damage questions

What should I do first after water reaches carpet or flooring?

Stop the source if it is safe to do so, move valuables out of the wet zone, and avoid walking through contaminated water. Call for extraction as soon as the area is safe to enter—waiting increases secondary damage.

When should professional help be called?

As soon as standing water or soaked carpet is present and the leak is under control. The earlier bulk water is removed, the higher the chance materials can be saved.

What happens to wet carpet?

We extract standing water, then dry and monitor. Salvageable carpet can often be cleaned; padding and heavily saturated areas may not be savable. We document what can stay and what cannot.

When is replacement necessary?

When materials stay wet too long, swell, delaminate, or hold contamination that cleaning cannot reverse. We are direct when replacement is the better path.

What information should I document?

Photos of the source and wet areas, approximate timing of the event, and any steps already taken help with your own records. For installed carpet recovery after the emergency phase, see also carpet cleaning and carpet stretching if buckling develops after drying.
