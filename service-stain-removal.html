The problem

Surface sprays don't always reach the pad.

Pet accidents often soak past the carpet face into the backing or padding. That is why odor returns when humidity rises even after the visible spot looks clean.

Store products treat what you can see. Professional pet odor work aims at what you can smell—deeper in the fiber structure—while being honest when contamination has gone further than one cleaning can fully reverse.

What we treat

Common pet accident types

Urine in carpet

The most common request. Severity depends on how much liquid reached the pad and how long it sat.

Other organic stains

Vomit, feces, and food-related accidents need different chemistry than simple dirt. We adjust treatment to the stain type when possible.

Area rugs

Rugs can hold odor in the pile and foundation. See also our area rug cleaning page when the whole rug needs cleaning, not only a spot.

Repeated accidents

Multiple old incidents in the same room are harder than a single fresh spot. Results vary; we set expectations before work starts.

How we work

Treat the source, then extract

Source-focused treatment

Good to know

Pet odor and stain questions

Can every pet odor be removed in one visit?

Many situations improve a lot in one visit. Severe or old contamination in the pad may need more work—or may not fully clear with cleaning alone.

When does replacement make more sense?

When urine has heavily saturated padding or subfloor, or when the same areas have been soaked repeatedly for a long time, replacement can be more effective than repeated cleanings. We will be direct if that is the case.

Is it safe for pets?

We use products suitable for homes with pets. Keep pets off the area until it is dry.

Do you guarantee 100% odor removal?

No. We work to maximize results and stay honest about limits. Guaranteeing zero odor in every case would not be truthful.

Is this the same as regular carpet cleaning?

No. Carpet cleaning addresses overall soil. Pet odor service targets organic staining and smell. Many homes need both in different areas—see carpet cleaning for whole-room cleaning.
