Carpet cleaning questions

How long until I can walk on the carpet?

Most rooms are usable the same day with care. Full dry time depends on humidity and airflow.

Is it safe for kids and pets?

We use products chosen for household use. Once dry, normal traffic is fine. Ask about specifics when you book.

Do you move furniture?

We discuss access when you book. Heavy items may need to be moved by you first.
