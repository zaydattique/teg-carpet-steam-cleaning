Good to know

Carpet Cleaning questions we get most

How long does carpet cleaning take?

A typical home (3 bedrooms) takes 1.5 to 2.5 hours. Commercial spaces vary by size. We'll give you a realistic estimate when you book.

How soon can I walk on the carpet?

You can walk immediately with clean socks or shoe covers. Wait 4–6 hours before replacing furniture and 8–12 hours before heavy traffic.

Does carpet cleaning remove pet odors?

Standard cleaning reduces odors significantly. For deep pet odor (urine in padding), we recommend our dedicated Pet Odor & Stain Removal service.
