Area Rug Cleaning

Area rugs cleaned properly — not just vacuumed.

Area rugs collect tracked-in soil, pet hair, and dust mites deep in the pile. We clean them with methods matched to the fiber so they last longer and look new again.

The problem before T.E.G

Surface vacuuming never reaches the base.

Rugs trap grit between the pile and the foundation. Walking on that grit wears fibers from the inside. Vacuuming the top looks fine for a week — then the dull look and odor return.

Without a proper clean, dyes can migrate, edges fray, and odors set in. That's the gap we close.

Our method

Fiber-aware cleaning, not one-size foam

We identify construction and fiber first, then choose the process: controlled extraction, gentle agitation, or specialized wash for delicate pieces — so color and backing stay intact.

Fiber identification

Wool, silk, synthetic, and blends get different handling.

Deep soil removal

Grit and allergens come out of the foundation, not just the tips.

Odor & stain treatment

Pet and food odors treated at the source.

Careful drying

Flat, air-circulated drying to protect shape and edges.

Our process

Step by step, on site

01

Inspection

We note fiber type, dyes, fringe, and problem spots before any water touches the rug.

02

Dusting & pre-treat

Dry soil is removed first; stains get targeted pre-treatment.

03

Wash / extraction

Controlled cleaning matched to the rug — not a carpet machine run on high.

04

Dry & finish

Proper drying, edge check, and return ready to place back in the room.
