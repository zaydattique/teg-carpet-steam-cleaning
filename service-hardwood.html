The problem before T.E.G

Dirty water and harsh products dull wood fast.

Steam mops and excess water can swell boards and cloud the finish. Grocery-store cleaners leave residue that attracts more dirt. Over time floors look gray even when you clean weekly.

Professional hardwood cleaning uses controlled moisture and the right chemistry for sealed wood.

Our method

Clean for wood — not for tile

We remove loose grit first, then clean with products and methods suited to finished hardwood. No standing water. Optional conditioning where appropriate to restore depth of color.

Grit removal first

Abrasive soil is lifted before it scratches under the pad.

Controlled moisture

No flooding — finish and joints stay protected.

Residue-free clean

No sticky film that re-attracts dirt.

Optional condition

Restore richness on worn high-traffic paths when suitable.

Good to know

What we don't do

We don't sand or refinish floors — that's a separate trade. We deep-clean finished hardwood so it looks its best between refinishes. Unfinished or waxed floors need different care; tell us the finish type when you book.
