The problem

Sprays don't deep-clean fabric.

Flipping cushions and using store sprays may mask a problem for a short time. Upholstery still holds dust, oils, food particles, and allergens in the fibers and along seams.

Sofas, sectionals, loveseats, and upholstered chairs in living rooms and waiting areas take daily contact. Pet soiling and repeated spills make the issue worse when only the surface is treated.

What we clean

Common upholstery we work on

Sofas and sectionals

Including large L-shapes and pieces with multiple cushions—scope is based on size and access.

Cushions and seats

Removable cushions can often be cleaned more thoroughly when both faces and edges are reachable.

Fabric types

Synthetic blends and many common household fabrics are typical candidates. We check labels and fabric condition before choosing a method.

Microfiber

Microfiber can look clean while holding oils and water marks. It needs a controlled approach so the nap and color stay even.

We focus on fabric upholstery. Leather is a different material—ask when you call; it is not treated the same way as cloth.

How we work

Fabric-aware cleaning, not one method for every sofa

We identify the fabric as far as labels and inspection allow, pre-treat spots, and use extraction suited to upholstery so we clean without soaking the frame or leaving a sticky residue.

Fabric check

Material, wear, and any cleaning-code tags guide how aggressive we can be.

Spot and soil treatment

Arms, seat centers, and visible stains get focused attention before overall cleaning.

Controlled extraction

Moisture is managed so cushions dry in a reasonable window and the wood or frame underneath is not over-wet.

Pet-related soiling

When odor is the main issue, we may recommend pairing furniture cleaning with our pet odor and stain approach for carpets or rugs in the same room.

Good to know

Upholstery cleaning questions

How long until I can use the sofa?

Often several hours; we'll tell you based on conditions on site, including fabric and humidity.

Can you clean leather?

We focus on fabric upholstery. Leather needs different products and care—ask when you call so we can set accurate expectations.

Will colors run?

We test when needed and adjust methods. Heavily worn or previously damaged fabric can still show limits; we discuss that before proceeding.

Can you clean a sectional?

Yes, sectionals are common requests. Quote depends on size, number of pieces, and whether cushions are removable.

What about pet odor on the couch?

Fabric cleaning helps when soil and odor are in the upholstery. If the smell is also in the carpet or pad underneath, address that with pet odor and stain removal as well.

Do you clean office or waiting-room chairs?

Fabric chairs and benches can be included. For full commercial carpet programs, see commercial carpet cleaning.
