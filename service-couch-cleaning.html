The problem before T.E.G

Upholstery holds soil and odor in the fiber.

Daily sitting, pets, and spills push dirt into cushions and fabric. Surface sprays mask problems temporarily. A proper clean extracts soil and treats odors when the material allows.

Our method

Matched to the fabric

We check labels and fiber type first. Some fabrics need low-moisture methods; others tolerate controlled extraction. We choose the safer effective approach for your piece.

Upholstery questions

Can all fabrics be cleaned the same way?

No. We match method to the material. Some delicate fabrics need specialty care we will discuss before starting.

Will cushions stay damp long?

Dry time depends on fabric and method. We aim for controlled moisture and give clear guidance.

Do you clean dining chairs and sectionals?

Yes — sofas, chairs, sectionals, and similar upholstered pieces.
