About T.E.G

A Milwaukee cleaning team built on clear prices and thorough work

We don’t sell mystery packages. You get a defined scope, a number before we start, and methods matched to the surface — carpet, tile, upholstery, rugs, and more.

Who we are

T.E.G Carpet & Furniture Steam Cleaning operates from 4111 N Port Washington Rd suite 1, Milwaukee, WI 53217. We serve the city and western suburbs: Wauwatosa, Brookfield, New Berlin, West Allis, Greenfield, Franklin, Muskego, Pewaukee, Oak Creek, Elm Grove, Hales Corners, and Greendale.

Our work covers ten services — from standard carpet hot water extraction to pet odor treatment, commercial floors, water extraction after leaks, carpet stretching, and finish-safe hardwood cleaning. One team, one phone number, one standard: explain the plan, then deliver it.

We are available 24/7 for booking and emergency needs. Licensed and insured. Products chosen for effectiveness and household safety once dry.

How we work

  1. Listen first — rooms, traffic, pets, water events, and what “done” means to you.
  2. Quote before machines — a clear number and scope, not “we’ll see when we’re finished.”
  3. Match method to surface — hot water extraction for carpet, controlled moisture for hardwood, fiber-aware care for rugs.
  4. Leave the space usable — grooming, drying guidance, and an honest note if a stain has limits.
