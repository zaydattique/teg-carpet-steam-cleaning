Safety, Method & Drying

Are products safe for children and pets?

Yes. Professional solutions that are safe for kids and pets once dry. Tell us about allergies when you book.

What method do you use?

Hot water extraction (steam cleaning) — the approach most carpet manufacturers recommend for deep cleaning.

How long until carpet is dry?

Typically 4–8 hours depending on thickness, humidity, and airflow. Fans and ventilation help.
