The problem

Bonnet cleaning isn't deep cleaning.

Many commercial maintenance programs rely on surface methods that brighten the top of the carpet but leave soil lower in the pile. High-traffic lobbies, corridors, and open work areas need extraction-level cleaning on a schedule that matches how the space is used.

In the Milwaukee area, tracked-in grit and winter road salt accelerate wear at entrances and along main paths. Those zones usually need more attention than quiet offices farther inside the building.

Where this applies

Common commercial settings

Commercial carpet problems show up in a wide range of buildings. The same core approach applies, but priorities change by use:

Offices and open floors

Desk areas, conference rooms, and shared corridors collect soil from daily traffic. Cleaning is often scheduled outside peak work hours.

Retail and customer-facing spaces

Entrances and aisles take the most abuse. Appearance and odor matter because customers notice the floor first.

Property and facilities teams

Multi-unit or multi-floor sites benefit from a clear scope: which areas, which frequency, and what is included before work starts.

High-wear zones

We prioritize lobbies, elevator landings, and main walkways when a full-building clean is not needed in one visit.

If your building also has upholstered seating in lobbies or waiting areas, that can be discussed as an add-on through our upholstery cleaning service.

Good to know

Commercial cleaning questions

Can you work nights or weekends?

May be available depending on schedule—ask when you request a quote so we can plan access and drying time.

Do you clean office chairs and lobby seating?

Upholstery can be added when needed. Tell us what pieces are included so the quote covers them. See upholstery cleaning for fabric-focused work.

How often should commercial carpet be cleaned?

It depends on traffic. Many offices benefit from a deeper clean on a recurring schedule; entrances may need attention more often than low-use rooms.

Is this the same as residential carpet cleaning?

The cleaning principles overlap, but commercial work is scoped around access, foot traffic, and business hours. Homes are covered on our carpet cleaning page.

How do you handle winter salt and grit?

Entrances and main paths take the most tracked-in soil in winter. We can focus scope on those zones when a full-floor clean is not required.
