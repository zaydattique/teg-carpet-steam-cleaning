Good to know

Steam cleaning questions

Is steam cleaning the same as carpet cleaning?

Many people use the terms interchangeably. Our steam / hot water extraction is the professional method for deep carpet cleaning and is also used on suitable hard floors when appropriate.

How long until I can walk on the floor?

Most rooms are usable the same day with care. Full dry time depends on humidity, airflow, and how wet the surface was before cleaning.

Is it safe for kids and pets?

We use products chosen for household use. Once dry, treated areas are generally fine for normal traffic. Ask about specific product concerns when you book.
