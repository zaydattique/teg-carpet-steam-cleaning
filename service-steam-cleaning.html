How this fits

Steam cleaning and carpet cleaning—related, not identical

On our site, steam cleaning refers to professional hot-water / heat-assisted cleaning aimed at a deeper reset of suitable carpets and hard surfaces. Carpet cleaning is the dedicated service page for wall-to-wall carpet: inspection, pre-treatment, extraction, and finishing focused specifically on carpet fibers.

In everyday language, people often say “steam clean” when they mean professional hot-water extraction. We keep both pages so you can start from the term you searched—then we match the method to the surface and soil after we understand the job.

When it helps

When steam cleaning is a good fit

Light vacuuming and mopping maintain day-to-day appearance. They do not always reach embedded soil or support the level of hygiene some households and workplaces want.

Deeper soil release

Heat and moisture help loosen soil so it can be extracted rather than pushed around.

Hygiene-focused requests

Useful when you want more than a cosmetic surface clean after heavy use, pets, or long intervals between deep cleans.

Carpets and suitable floors

Most often requested for carpet; we also evaluate tile and other hard surfaces case by case. For dedicated tile work, see tile & grout cleaning.

Clear expectations

We explain what the visit will cover and what it will not—especially for delicate fibers or severe odor problems.

Good to know

Steam cleaning questions

Is steam the same as your carpet cleaning service?

They are related services with overlapping goals. Use this page if you searched for steam cleaning; use the carpet cleaning page for wall-to-wall detail. At the appointment, we match the method to the surface.

Is steam safe for all carpets?

Most synthetic carpets are good candidates. Delicate or natural fibers need assessment first so we do not over-wet or stress the material.

How wet will floors be afterward?

We extract aggressively. Dry times are often several hours; we'll tell you based on conditions on site.

Can steam remove pet urine odors?

It can help with surface soil and some odor. Severe or old urine problems often need targeted pet odor and stain treatment beyond a standard steam clean.

Do you steam hardwood?

Hardwood needs controlled moisture—not flooding. For finished wood, see our hardwood floor cleaning page rather than assuming a carpet-style steam process.
