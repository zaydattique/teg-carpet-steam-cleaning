Process

How our carpet cleaning works

A plain-language walkthrough of what happens on a T.E.G job in Milwaukee — so you know what you’re booking.

1. Inspection & upfront price

We confirm rooms, fiber type, problem areas, and access. You get a price before machines run. No “we’ll see when we’re done” billing.

2. Pre-treatment

Traffic lanes and visible spots get pre-treatment so extraction can lift more soil in fewer passes.

3. Hot water extraction

Also called steam cleaning by many homeowners: heated water and solution into the pile, then strong recovery of dirty water. This is the method most manufacturers recommend for deep cleaning versus dusting the tips of the yarn.

4. Detail work & drying

Edges, stairs, and stubborn spots get attention. Fibers are groomed. Typical dry time is 4–8 hours; airflow helps. We tell you if a stain has chemical or age limits.
