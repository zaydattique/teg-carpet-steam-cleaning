Good to know

When stretching works

Best results when the carpet fiber is still sound and pad isn't collapsed or soaked. Severe pet damage, rot, or multiple failed seams may need replacement — we'll tell you up front with a clear price either way.

Good to know

Carpet stretching questions

Can carpet be stretched after cleaning?

Sometimes. Cleaning does not always cause wrinkles, but loose carpet can become more noticeable after a deep clean. Stretching is a separate service when the carpet has lost tension on the tack strip.

Why does carpet become loose or develop wrinkles?

Age, humidity changes, furniture moves, poor original install, and long-term traffic can all reduce tension. Buckles are not only cosmetic—they are trip hazards.

When is stretching possible?

When the carpet fiber and pad are still sound enough to re-tension. Collapsed pad, rot, or severe damage may mean replacement is the better option.

When does carpet need replacement instead?

When seams have failed repeatedly, the carpet is torn, or the pad is ruined. We assess before recommending stretch versus replace.

Can damaged seams be repaired?

Many open or peaked seams can be re-bonded when the surrounding carpet is intact. We include seam and edge work in the assessment when those problems are present.

Is this part of regular carpet cleaning?

No. Cleaning and stretching are different services. For soil and appearance, see carpet cleaning.
