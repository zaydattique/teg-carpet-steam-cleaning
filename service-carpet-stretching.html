The problem before T.E.G

Humidity, age, and bad installs loosen carpet.

Carpet expands and contracts. Over time — or after water events — it can ripple, bubble, or pull away from the tack strip. Walking on loose carpet is unsafe and accelerates wear.

Our method

Power stretch, not knee kicks alone

We use proper stretching tools to put tension across the room and reseat edges where needed. Spot knee-kicking is not enough for large loose areas.

Full-room tension

Stretch from the right direction so waves flatten out.

Edge reseat

Carpet is hooked back onto tack strip where it has pulled free.

Seam check

We note open seams that may need separate repair.

Safer walk surface

Goal is a flat, secure floor again.

Good to know

Carpet stretching questions

Can all buckling be fixed by stretching?

Most humidity and install-related wrinkles can. Severe water damage, delaminated backing, or destroyed tack strip may need replacement instead.

Do you move furniture?

We discuss access when you book. Heavy or fragile items may need to be moved by you first.

Is stretching the same as restretch after cleaning?

Sometimes carpet loosens after cleaning if it was already marginal. Stretching is a separate service when the install has gone slack.
